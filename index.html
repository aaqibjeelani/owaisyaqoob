The story

From Pulwama's saffron fields
to the Octagon

Owais Yaqoob was born in 1998 in Murran, a village in Pulwama district — the heart of India's saffron country, in south Kashmir. He started training in taekwondo at 15 and spent the next decade collecting 11 gold and 6 silver medals on the national circuit, before turning to mixed martial arts and winning three national amateur titles.

To build a fighter capable of competing internationally, he left home to train wherever the best coaches were: wrestling and grappling in Chandigarh, striking at Sumo Fight Club in Bali, and two months at the Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov School in Dagestan under Islam Makhachev. That road eventually led him to the American Kickboxing Academy in California, where he now trains full-time under Javier Mendez and Khabib Nurmagomedov as part of Team Khabib.

"Alhamdulillah! Thank you Bulgaria for not supporting me — one day you will love me." Owais Yaqoob, after knocking out Delyan Georgiev at BRAVE CF 107, Aug 1 2026

In October 2025, Khabib himself publicly praised Owais's progress in the gym — a rare endorsement for a fighter still building his record. Since his BRAVE CF debut in September 2025, Owais has gone 3–0 inside the promotion, finishing all three opponents, and has become the first fighter from Jammu & Kashmir to win inside BRAVE CF.
